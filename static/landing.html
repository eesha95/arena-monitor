What you get

One page instead of twenty tabs. Every tile says why it matters, and you can minimise or maximise any tile.

🗺️

Live world map

League matches at their home studios, places named in the news, Valve's game-server relays, and earthquakes near them.

⚔️

Matches and results

Live and finished pro matches from the official League of Legends feed and OpenDota, with a calendar of what's next.

📈

Player counts and forecasts

Concurrent Steam players for the big competitive titles. Next-hour forecasts appear only when they beat a simple baseline in a backtest.

⚡

Signals, not noise

Headlines tagged as roster moves, results, patches, business deals, releases, bans and outages, so you can filter to what you need.

⭐

Watchlist and brief

Follow teams, players or companies. Your watchlist hits rank higher in a top-5 brief, and every item is cited.

🎛️

Views for your role

One click switches between Fan, Team / org, Publisher / analyst, Sponsor / brand and Ops views, or you can save your own.

Sponsors and brands

Which games are rising, and which events are coming up.

How it works

  1. 1

    Sign in

    Use Google, Discord or a one-time code sent to your email.

  2. 2

    Pick a view

    Choose your role's view, add teams to your watchlist, and filter by game, region and time.

  3. 3

    Act on cited signals

    Open any item at its source. Numbers on screen come from live checks, and estimates are labelled.

Where the data comes from

Public sources only, each with its terms noted. Headlines link to their publishers. Arena Monitor does not copy articles.

  • Steam Web API (not endorsed by Valve) · Epic Games Store · CheapShark
